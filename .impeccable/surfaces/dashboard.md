## Direction contract

THESIS: The corpus is a naturalist's cabinet of specimens, not a SaaS dashboard — every paper carries full provenance, and the interface refuses the shadow-card, stat-tile default this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Cream/bone ground, one deep ink (iron-gall brown), printed rules and corner brackets standing in for drawer dividers, a fixed-width field-hand mono for IDs/counts paired with a quiet text serif for prose. No soft shadows, no rounded stat tiles, no floating cards.

STORY: The visitor understands their corpus as a physical cabinet of catalogued specimens; starting a topic is launching an expedition whose field log appends live, stage by stage, proving the system is doing real, inspectable work rather than hiding behind a spinner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dashboard opens on the cabinet — drawer rows (collections) with specimen counts at left, a live field-log panel at right showing the most recent or active expedition; the primary "start an expedition" action sits above the fold. New Topic transforms this same surface in place into the live log on submit, never navigating away.

FORM: Herbarium / Specimen Ledger, candidate 6 of the ranked list, seed key de13333b, mode operate, build path code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
